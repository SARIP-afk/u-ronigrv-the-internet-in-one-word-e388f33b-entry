A program (Node 22, no dependencies) that writes one word for the whole
internet to `/output/entry.txt` — the single word that describes the entire
internet.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

## The word

> Actually

8 characters, 8 bytes, no trailing newline.

## Why this word wins against the judge brief

The judge weighs, most important first: one word on the subject; funny;
fits the whole internet; surprising; lands in one beat.

- **One word.** A single token, eight characters — no space, no hyphen,
  nothing for the first criterion to trip on.
- **Funny, a character — not a label.** It is not "chaos" or "memes". Read
  it and you hear "Well, actually…" — the pedantic corrector, the internet's
  mascot. Anyone who has ever argued online recognises that person in one beat.
- **Fits the whole internet.** Correction culture lives on every platform,
  every topic, every language — not one app, not one mood.
- **Surprising.** It is not the first obvious word anyone reaches for; people
  reach for "chaos", "cats" or "doomscroll" first.
- **Lands on its own.** No explanation needed for the joke to work.

Actually won an 8-candidate head-to-head (Buffering, Cookies, Captcha, WiFi,
Tabs, Lurking, Unsubscribe) by sweeping all five criteria; the full
comparison is in `u-ronigrv-the-internet-in-one-word-e388f33b-candidates.md`.
